## Track A: Traditional Judging (Required for finalist consideration)

### What to Submit:

**1. Concept of Operations (ConOps)** - ~1,000 words max
- Word or PDF format
- **Must be CUI-free** — sanitize all client/project details
- Structure: Problem → Solution → Impact
- Include value case: time savings, cost reduction, mission impact
- Use ConOps template (see below)

**2. Demo Video** - 2-3 minutes
- **Talking head:** Explain your build, problem, solution, impact
- **Bonus:** Include architecture diagram walkthrough with sanitized screenshots/mockups
- **NO screen recording of live agent with client or sensitive data. Dummy data and data accessible to all AFS is ok.**
- Clear audio matters more than Hollywood quality
- MP4 format

**Suggested 2-minute breakdown:**
- 0:00-0:30: Problem statement (who has it, why it matters)
- 0:30-1:20: Solution walkthrough (what it does, how it works)
- 1:20-1:50: Impact & value case (time/cost/mission improvement)
- 1:50-2:00: Future state (where this could go)

**How to record your video:**

**Option 1: Microsoft Stream (Camera + Teleprompter)**
- Go to the [Stream start page](https://www.office365.us/launch/stream?auth=2)
- Select "Camera recording" to open the camera
- When prompted, select "Allow" to enable your camera and microphone
- Choose your webcam and microphone settings
- Optional: click the teleprompter icon and paste your script
- Click the circular capture button to begin or pause recording
- Select "Review" in the bottom right-hand corner
- Review your recording and click "Finish" (or re-record if needed)
- After uploading, enhance and share your video in the Stream web app
- Select "Share" to create a sharing link

**Option 2: Kaltura Capture (Media Exchange)**
- Open Media Exchange
- Click "+ Add New"
- Choose "Kaltura Capture" and start recording
- Watch it once after recording to confirm audio is clear

**Option 3: Windows Snipping Tool (Quick & Easy)**
- Press Windows Key + Shift + S to open Snipping Tool
- Click the camera icon (screen recording)
- Select the area you want to record
- Unmute your microphone
- Unmute system audio (if needed for demos)
- Click Record
- Save as MP4
- Watch it once after recording to confirm audio is clear

**Option 4: Teams Meeting (With Your Face)**
- Open a Teams call
- Turn on your camera
- Share your screen (if showing diagrams/mockups)
- Click Record
- Speak through your problem, solution, impact
- Stop recording when done
- Download MP4 from Teams
- Watch it once after recording to confirm audio is clear

## ConOps Template (~1,000 Words Max)

Copy/paste this into your Word/PDF and fill it out:

### PROBLEM STATEMENT
What problem does this solve? Who experiences it? Why does it matter to the mission?

### SOLUTION
How does your build solve the problem?
What features are functional right now?
What can you prove today?

### IMPACT & VALUE CASE
What is the bottom-line value? (Choose what applies and be specific)
- **Time savings:** "Reduces X hours to Y minutes per week"
- **Cost reduction:** "Saves $X annually by eliminating manual process"
- **Mission effectiveness:** "Increases operational tempo by X%"
- **Mission lethality:** "Accelerates targeting cycle by X hours"

How would you measure success?
What changes if this is deployed?

### FUTURE STATE (Optional)
Where could this go next?
What additional systems/data would you connect?
What's the moonshot version?

## Example ConOps:

### PROBLEM STATEMENT
AFS analysts supporting logistics operations spend 4-6 hours per week manually extracting data from unstructured mission reports to populate status dashboards. This delays leadership visibility by 24-48 hours and creates bottlenecks during surge operations, reducing operational tempo and response effectiveness.

### SOLUTION
Mission Report Parser uses Claude on GenAI Hub to ingest PDF/Word mission reports and extract key data points (objectives, timelines, resources, risks). The agent currently:
- Parses 20+ report formats (SITREP, OPREP, AAR) using structured output extraction
- Extracts mission objectives, timelines, personnel counts, equipment status, and operational risks
- Outputs structured JSON that analysts copy/paste into dashboards
- Validates extracted data against mission templates and flags anomalies for human review

This reduces manual extraction from 4-6 hours to 30 minutes per week per analyst.

### IMPACT & VALUE CASE
**Time Savings:** Reduces manual data entry from 4-6 hours to 30 minutes per week per analyst—saving 20+ analyst hours per week across a 5-person team.

**Mission Effectiveness:** Accelerates leadership visibility by 24-48 hours during surge operations, enabling faster decision cycles and improved operational tempo.

**Cost Reduction:** Analyst time savings translate to $50K+ annually in labor cost avoidance (based on 20 hours/week at average analyst fully-loaded rate).

**Success Metrics:** Time to dashboard update, error rate in extracted data, leadership satisfaction with visibility, operational decision cycle time.

### FUTURE STATE
With API connectors and system integrations, Mission Report Parser would:
- Auto-populate Power BI dashboards via API (eliminating copy/paste step)
- Connect to mission planning systems for real-time status updates
- Integrate with supply chain databases to cross-reference equipment availability
- Trigger automated alerts to leadership when critical thresholds are breached

The moonshot: A fully autonomous mission operations dashboard that ingests reports, validates data, populates visualizations, detects anomalies, and recommends courses of action—all without analyst intervention.
