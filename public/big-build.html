5 · Requirements assessment

What does this notice mean for our project?

  1. Official funding notice and attachments
  2. Structured requirements, each linked to its source
  3. Checked against the project facts you give
  4. Preliminary findings and targeted questions

Checked by code

Explicit conditions such as a funding limit or a matching requirement. The numbers are compared to the stated rule, with no model guessing.

Interpreted by the model, always provisional

Requirements that depend on nuanced language and project context. The application controls the permitted findings and keeps the uncertainty.

Evidence and questions

Each finding links to the official source. Missing information becomes a follow-up question, not an invented answer.

  • Appears supported
  • Possible conflict
  • Preparation gap
  • Unknown

Unknown is not a rejection. A preparation gap is not an eligibility conflict. Everything is preliminary and for human review.

Assumptions and arithmetic
  • Per assessment: manual 6–10 h (midpoint 8 h) vs. projected 2–3 h (midpoint 2.5 h). Difference 5.5 h, or 68.75%, shown as ~70%.
  • One round: 300 applications × 50% adoption = 150 assisted assessments; 150 × 5.5 h = 825 h, shown as ~800 h.
  • The 300-application round is an illustrative scenario, not an average across programs.
  • Hours freed are potential capacity, not automatically budget savings. Preparation and verification overhead is not yet measured. These estimates need validation.
  • Federal side, hypothesis only: 150 assisted applicants × 40% assumed clarification incidence × 25% avoided × 1 h = 15 h under simplifying assumptions.
